This is the corner of the site for the work around the work: what I am learning, details worth remembering, and ideas that are still taking shape.

The format is intentionally simple. New entries can include images, links, headings, and lists - then become part of this notebook when published.

## What belongs here

- A lesson from building a data pipeline
- A project decision that changed the outcome
- A useful visualisation, book, place, or curiosity

The goal is to keep a record of the thinking that sits behind the code.
